# Telehealth-Cybersecurity-Risk-Assessment
This project is to develop a cybersecurity risk register for a telehealth company to align with HIPAA and NIST RMF.


### 🚀 Scenario Overview

#### Company: 
SecurePulse (Mock Telehealth Startup)

#### Size: 
10 Employees (Remote)

#### Industry: 
Healthcare Technology

#### Mission: 
Provide secure, real-time video consultations between doctors and patients while maintaining strict HIPAA compliance and protecting Patient Health Information (PHI).

#### Objective
To transition the organization from an ad-hoc security posture to a formal, risk-based management framework. This project demonstrates the identification of business-critical risks, the quantification of their impact, and the strategic selection of controls to reduce residual risk.


### 🔨 Methodology & Frameworks
1. Asset Inventory
2. Risk Identification
3. Risk Analysis
4. Risk Treatment
5. Residual Risk Assessment
